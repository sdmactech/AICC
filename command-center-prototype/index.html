Governance & Human Approval Rules
Agents may execute low-risk tasks automatically. Sensitive actions route here first.

Always Requires Approval

Spending above set budget/profit reinvestment policy, contacting clients, changing client systems, sending emails, buying domains, deploying code, accessing sensitive data, changing Microsoft 365 settings.

Council Review First

Strategic pivots, legal ambiguity, brand risk, security-sensitive decisions, new business launches, major operational changes, and anything with high downside risk.

Auto-Execute Allowed

Internal notes, drafts, summaries, diagnostics, classification, report creation, task routing, status tagging, non-destructive checks, and low-risk internal workflow updates.
